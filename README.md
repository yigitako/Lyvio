# Lyvio
## Overview

**Lyvio** is an intelligent music companion that transforms how you discover and enjoy music.
 Rather than searching through endless genres or artists, Lyvio will detect your emotional mood and
instantly curate a personalized playlist that resonates with your emotional state.
Whether you need uplifting tracks to boost your energy, calming melodies to unwind after a long day,
or reflective songs that match your contemplative mood, Lyvio understands the emotional landscape of
music and delivers the perfect soundtrack for every moment of your life.
